# Robustness

Inspect pre-allocation behavioral-support composition, baseline-family weighting and shrinkage, K-ranking stability and spatial-proxy parameter settings.

## Interpretation

Baseline-ablation means use their recorded complete-case cohorts. S2b shares are ratios of mean absolute dividend masses across BO1, BO2 and higher orders. These are distinct from post-allocation BO1/BO2 shares. The displayed parameter analysis evaluates spatial responses. Endpoint-difference sensitivity and numerical Harsanyi reconstruction residuals have different definitions.
