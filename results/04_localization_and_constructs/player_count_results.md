## D12. Single-vehicle BRT players

Only BRT players with one member vehicle are retained and reranked within scenes.
Their original D and response values are used; the loss game and responses are
not recalculated on the filtered player set. Physical ranking uses the maximum
physical relevance among a player's members. Random scores are frozen on the full
candidate set before filtering. Evaluation requires at least two retained players;
valid scene counts remain metric-specific. The @3 budget is min(3,n).

All 48 paired differences have positive lower confidence bounds and BH q < 0.05.
The BH family contains 16 tests per model (two responses × four metrics × two
comparators), including any constant-metric comparisons. Intervals use 2,000
scene-bootstrap replicates. Paired means use scenes valid for both rankings.

## D13. Candidate-count strata

The 2, 3, 4 and ≥5 strata use all BRT players in the D/R cohort before singleton
filtering. Metrics are evaluated within strata and averaged with equal scene weights.
The comparator and random-score rules are the same as in D12. For two or three
candidates, Hit@3 is identically one and its paired differences are zero. Those
24 zero differences are retained below and in the 64-test BH family per model.
The other 168 differences have positive lower confidence bounds and BH q < 0.05.

## D14. Historical-proximity adjustment at ≥5 and ≥8 players

D and each response are rank-regressed on both historical proximity measures
within each scene. Residual correlation defines partial Spearman. Eligibility
requires finite, nonconstant D, Rs, Rt, Hs and Ht, a full-rank control matrix
(intercept, Hs ranks, Ht ranks), and centered residual norms above 1e-10 for D, Rs
and Rt. Raw, adjusted and raw-minus-adjusted estimates use the same valid scenes
within each threshold. The ≥5 and ≥8 samples are nested; their means are not
subtracted for paired inference.

This threshold-sensitivity table uses the D14 resampling record. Its ≥5 point
estimates and counts agree with S5d; its independently resampled confidence
intervals can differ from the S5d/Figure 7d ≥5 intervals. Those paper intervals
are shown separately in [Common analysis sets](common_analysis_sets.md#panel-d-historical-proximity-adjustment).
Each model has separate four-test BH families for adjusted means and
raw-minus-adjusted differences. All intervals use 2,000 scene-bootstrap replicates.
