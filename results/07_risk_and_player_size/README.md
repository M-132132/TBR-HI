# Risk and player size

Selected tables show candidate-count-standardized high-risk-minus-control localization effects and EMP-M player-size composition.

## Interpretation

Negative adjusted effects mean lower localization in the high-risk group. Status fields identify estimable comparisons. This traffic-state comparison differs from comparing D with reference rankings within a candidate-count stratum. The latter three-model analysis, and the single-vehicle sensitivity analysis, are in [Player count and size](../04_localization_and_constructs/player_count_results.md). Player-size composition is EMP-M only.
