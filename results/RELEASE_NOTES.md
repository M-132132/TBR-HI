# Results coverage

## Presentation scope

The results collection contains selected aggregate Markdown tables and two
overview figures (PNG and PDF). It includes no CSV exports, CSV manifests,
per-scene records or result-rendering scripts. The eight topic guides describe
the contents of each displayed page. Main-text Table 2 entity-localization
method-comparison results are outside this collection.

Supplementary Data D12–D15 are represented by two additional readable pages:

- [Player size and candidate-count sensitivity](04_localization_and_constructs/player_count_results.md):
  all D12/D13 four-metric summaries and paired comparisons, coverage, and D14
  ≥5/≥8 adjusted statistics and same-scene raw-minus-adjusted differences.
- [Common analysis sets](04_localization_and_constructs/common_analysis_sets.md):
  all 126 D15 component estimates, 48 paired comparisons and 24 coverage records.

## Analysis conventions

- Dependence structure and response results come from formal source exports.
  Temporal and downstream construct analyses use the full-context temporal response.
- Aligned deletion/retention uses forecast rank 1, K=1 and no shrinkage.
  The main aligned table uses Phi and three comparison methods, as in S3b.
  Additional BO1 estimates, all remaining paired comparisons and the original
  five-representation retention figure are provided on a separate reading page. The
  original paired protocol uses 10 method pairs per model and metric.
- Original-loss reconstruction uses the full-scene analysis, with raw and robust
  BO1, BO1+BO2 and full representations. Raw and robust BO2 increments are shown.
- S2b uses pre-allocation absolute dividend masses at K=6, uniform weights and
  lambda=0 or 0.25. Shares divide each scene-mean mass by the total over all three
  support orders. Post-allocation BO1/BO2 shares have a different denominator.
- Historical adjustment, descriptive constructs, paired common-set constructs
  and fixed-budget populations have separate validity rules. Matching entities
  alone does not guarantee the same metric-valid scenes.
- D14 ≥5 intervals use its independent threshold-sensitivity resampling record.
  The Figure 7d/S5d ≥5 intervals are retained separately on the D15 page.
- Fixed-budget paired effects retain their original nine-test BH family.
  Random-tie intervals describe tie randomization, distinct from scene bootstrap.
- Quadrant agreement retains its source intervals, including 1,000-replicate
  legacy statistics. Principal response and paired analyses use 2,000 replicates.

## Precision and interpretation

Tables round source estimates and intervals for reading. Temporal BO2 versus
BO1-sum, conditional high sets, EMP-M player-size composition and EMP-M
size-standardized/singleton budgets retain archived display precision. Their
rounded values do not supply additional precision or unreported uncertainty.

BO2 increments are metric-specific. Traffic-state adjustment and budget/tie-policy
comparisons retain their stated populations and endpoints. D and R are model
dependence and response measures; they do not estimate accident probability.
The prediction-based P–G reference calibrates correspondence with future risk.
